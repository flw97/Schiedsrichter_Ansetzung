# Changed
- Toasts follow the active light and dark scheme: surface, text, border and status colors come from the Vuetify theme instead of vue3-toastify’s default black panel and bright green

```css
--toastify-color-light: var(--surface);
--toastify-color-dark: var(--surface);
--toastify-text-color-light: var(--ink);
--toastify-text-color-dark: var(--ink);
--toastify-color-success: rgb(var(--v-theme-success));
```
