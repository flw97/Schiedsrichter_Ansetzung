# Changed
- The app-bar menu edits the signed-in account instead of opening the overviews
 - Benutzer (`/mein-benutzer`, `PUT /api/me`): name, username, email, password. A new password needs the current one
 - Lizenzen (`/meine-lizenzen`, `PUT /api/me/license`): own licence level and validity
 - Standort (`/mein-standort`, `PUT /api/me/standort`): own address, with a map preview when the town is known
- The first save of a licence or address creates a referee profile if the account has none (`User::ensureReferee`), so the person appears in the left-hand lists
- `GET /api/state` includes `me.email`
- Meine Daten no longer edits name, email or address; those live in the account menu. Club, phone, availability and blackouts stay there
- Rollen, Lizenzen and Standorte in the drawer stay the overviews across everyone
